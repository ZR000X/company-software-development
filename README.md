# company-software-development

Documenting and designing business processes in the open, with an eye to co-designing a company.

## Inspiration: [The Unreasonable Effectiveness Of Plain Text](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=WgV6M1LyfNY)

Other concepts: Knowledge Gardening, Learning in Public

This repository aims to serve the purpose of documenting and designing business processes using insights from working in the field. The overall goal is collaboration with like-minded people who want to co-design a company.

## The need for process

While working within the industry, it has become increasingly, desperately apparent that designing how you work is so easy to overlook, defer to the future when things are "quieter", yet so essential to improving not only your quality of work, but quality of life, business performance, and all the other bottom lines.

The radical idea of designing a business entirely in the open then sprung up randomly one evening while driving home.

NOTE: AI is going to be used to write a lot of content in here via prompts.

## Tools

- AI tools are used for generating some of the content.

## Getting started

- **Principles** (why we think what we think—durable essays) are listed in reading order in [PRINCIPLES.md](PRINCIPLES.md). Individual essays live in [principles/](principles/).
- **Entity notes** (domain concepts: Epic, Project, Person, Team, etc.) live in [entities/](entities/). See [entities/README.md](entities/README.md) for naming and structure.
- **Process notes** (how work is done—steps, tools, data models) live in [processes/](processes/). See [processes/README.md](processes/README.md) for naming and structure.
- **Tool notes** (JIRA, Excel, SharePoint, Teams, etc.) live in [tools/](tools/). See [tools/README.md](tools/README.md) for naming and structure.
